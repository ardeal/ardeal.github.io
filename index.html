About Me

I received my Ph.D. in Communication and Information Systems from the Chinese Academy of Sciences and my Bachelor degree in Telecommunication Engineering from Wuhan University.

My research and engineering experience spans Deep Learning, Large Language Models (LLM), Edge AI, TinyML, Sparse AI, Embedded Machine Learning, Gesture Recognition, and low-power AI acceleration systems.

I previously worked at Intel Shanghai as a Senior Algorithms Engineer, focusing on wearable-device gesture recognition, deep learning algorithms, computer vision, and embedded AI optimization using Python and C/C++.

I have also led and commercialized multiple industrial AI systems across Moffett AI, Chaoyang Semiconductor, Shanghai Electric, and startup environments, including sparse neural network acceleration, AI chip optimization, subway infrastructure AI systems, and ultra-lightweight edge AI deployments.

I am available for academic reviewing and technical collaboration in Deep Learning, Machine Learning, NLP, Large Language Models, Attention mechanisms, Edge AI, TinyML, Sparse AI, and Embedded AI systems.

Selected Industrial Contributions

Moffett AI

Led sparse AI optimization and data architecture for sparse acceleration platforms. Developed block-based parameter separation algorithms combined with Knowledge Distillation, achieving significant acceleration for neural network training and inference. The sparse AI chips were integrated into high-performance servers from Inspur.
Chaoyang Semiconductor

Served as System AI Architect to optimize RAM sizing and power consumption for AI chips. The optimized chips were commercialized at large scale and deployed in products from Transsion, one of the largest smartphone vendors in Africa.
Shanghai Electric

Independently developed and productionized an automated subway meter recognition system. Manually optimized deep learning algorithms into pure C/C++ for Intel i3 CPUs without GPU acceleration. The production system was officially deployed in Shanghai Subway Line 5.
Solitorch (Co-founder & CTO)

Designed ultra-lightweight smart water meter recognition systems under strict edge-device power constraints. Compressed neural network size from 15M parameters down to 0.3M while maintaining 99.9% recognition accuracy. Successfully deployed onto low-cost AI edge chips.
